# Homepage statistics card refinement

## Scope
Update only the three existing statistics cards on the homepage. Preserve all numbers, labels, rotating records, card order, count-up timing, record timing, and surrounding sections.

## Changes
- Replace the cream surfaces with transparent deep olive liquid glass, including subtle reflections, layered depth, and restrained green shadows.
- Keep the current three-column layout but reduce card height and vertical spacing to match the compact proportions of the reference.
- Replace the pulse-only ECG treatment with a dark forest/olive neon waveform that travels continuously from left to right in a seamless loop.
- Keep the waveform lightweight and add restrained glow and dimensional depth without altering the rotating record cards.
- Preserve reduced-motion support by showing a static waveform when motion reduction is enabled.
- Adjust text and inner-card contrast only as needed for readability on the darker glass.

## Validation
Check desktop and mobile sizing, card compactness, ECG continuity, text contrast, overflow, reduced-motion behavior, and preview/build errors.

## Technical details
Use the existing statistics React markup and scoped Tailwind v4 CSS utilities only. No content, backend, navigation, or other homepage changes.
